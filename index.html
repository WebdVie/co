FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes a few days, a business website one to three weeks, and a store two to four weeks depending on content readiness.

Do I need to provide content?

If you have texts and images, great. If not, we guide you on what to prepare and help structure the pages.

Can I edit the website myself later?

Yes. We can build with an easy content dashboard, or handle updates for you after launch.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. We set up your domain, hosting and SSL so you don't have to deal with the technical side.

How does payment work?

We agree on a clear scope first, then work with an upfront deposit and the balance on delivery.
